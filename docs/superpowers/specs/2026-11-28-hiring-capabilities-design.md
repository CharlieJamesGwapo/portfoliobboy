## Approach and content boundaries

Expand the current evidence-backed skills explorer and automation section. A separate redesign or hundreds of generic badges would make the useful work harder to inspect. Keep the established typography, themes, mobile animation/accessibility improvements and on-demand optional features.

AWS Lambda, Docker/Linux deployment, GitHub/GitLab/Buddy CI/CD, data queues and operational/security practices have existing resume/project/repository evidence. Add missing technologies only where existing published evidence or the owner's explicit supplied platform list supports them. Do not invent AWS certifications, Kubernetes/Terraform depth, years, clients, quantified results or a larger completed-project count. Cloud/DevOps and bot questions in Alex must link to evidence and preserve uncertainty.

Automation entries describe service patterns the owner can discuss/build, not a verified count of deployed client workflows. Cover lead/CRM, communication and booking, RAG/agents, custom bots, document/data workflows, and AWS/DevOps automation. Each entry needs a business use, trigger, steps, expected qualitative outcome, integrations, and visible safeguards/implementation detail. Clearly label patterns and preserve client data privacy. No real bot accounts, workflows, voice sessions, contact messages or bookings are created by this content update.

## Interaction and presentation

Use a searchable, filterable catalogue with six initially visible cards and progressive disclosure, keeping phone pages manageable. Count matches dynamically, provide an empty result/reset path, and make enquiry actions stage an editable contact subject without sending. Skills should remain keyboard-accessible with clear group summaries and real case-study evidence links; AWS/DevOps must be discoverable. Follow both themes, reduced motion, narrow screens and existing navigation/fragment semantics.

Verify Alex/recruiter summaries do not turn generic solution patterns into past deliveries or certifications.
